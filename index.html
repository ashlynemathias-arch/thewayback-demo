Four access levels

Public, community member, Elder and administrator views each surface a different slice of the map, so sensitive knowledge only reaches the people meant to see it.

Elder review, every time

New places, photos and stories sit in a queue until an Elder confirms them. Nothing reaches the public map on its own.

OCAP, not an afterthought

Ownership, control, access and possession of what's submitted stay with the community, not with this tool.

Everything shown here is synthetic and illustrative, built for an Ivey MSc AFP capstone project.
